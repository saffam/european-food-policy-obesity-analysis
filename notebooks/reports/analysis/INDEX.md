## Main research question

What is the cross-country association between obesity prevalence
and selected policy indicators?

## Predictors

- Policy Count
- Instrument Diversity

## Outcome

- Obesity Prevalence

## Important interpretation

This is an exploratory observational analysis. The results should
not be interpreted as causal evidence.

## Main model

Obesity Prevalence ~ Policy Count + Instrument Diversity

## Sample

27 country-level observations.
