## Exploratory Policy and Obesity Analysis

A cross-country exploratory analysis was conducted to examine the
association between obesity prevalence and two policy indicators:

- Policy Count
- Instrument Diversity

The analysis includes 27 country-level observations.

An exploratory OLS model was estimated:

Obesity Prevalence ~ Policy Count + Instrument Diversity

The model produced an R-squared of 0.1965 and an adjusted
R-squared of 0.1296.

The analysis also includes Pearson and Spearman correlations,
regression diagnostics, confidence intervals and visualisations.

### Interpretation

The findings describe statistical associations in the analysed
cross-country dataset. They do not establish causal effects.

Country-level differences in obesity prevalence may reflect
demographic, socioeconomic, healthcare-system, environmental and
other factors not included in the model.
